# Area content review checklist

Everything listed here is in the database as **draft** — invisible to the
public — until someone signs it off. Publishing a destination page is a public
claim that Lavish Travels & Tours operates there, so the checks below are the
gate, not a formality.

Review in the admin panel at **`/admin/destinations`**. Work down a region,
publish what is correct, and leave the rest in draft.

## How to read the confidence column

- **High** — confirmed on the operator's own site, a tourism board, SANParks,
  CapeNature or SANBI.
- **Medium** — only found on travel directories. **Check these first.** They
  are the likeliest to be closed, renamed, or wrong about access.

Nothing in this batch states opening hours, prices or phone numbers. That is
deliberate: those go stale and a wrong price on a live page is a complaint.
Add them only if someone will keep them current.

> ### ⚠ Do not promise great white sharks at Gansbaai
>
> Great white sightings around Dyer Island have been scarce and unpredictable
> since roughly 2017, following orca predation on the local white sharks.
> Operators — including Marine Dynamics, on their own site — now largely show
> bronze whaler (copper) sharks instead. Marketing "great white shark cage
> diving" in Gansbaai today is a mis-selling risk with a real chance of a
> refund demand. The copy in the database says "marine wildlife trips" and
> names no operator, so you can pick your own supplier.
>
> Dyer Island and Shark Alley themselves are closed to the public, which is
> why they appear only inside this row rather than as visitable places.

under a specific area (draft, from the area seed):

| Landmark | Published under | Draft copy under |

| Boulders Beach Penguin Colony | Cape Town | Boulders Beach |

Publishing those five areas puts each landmark on two pages. Either is
defensible — a landmark can reasonably appear under both the city and the
neighbourhood — but it should be a decision. Say the word and the redundant
draft copies come out.

### 2. Prices are still placeholders

Unrelated to this content, but it has not changed: **every tour price in the
database is invented and the tours are live and bookable.** That is the one
thing on the site that could cost real money before it is fixed.
